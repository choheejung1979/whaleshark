8. Weather, Sea Conditions and Force Majeure

The program may be delayed, changed, rescheduled or cancelled due to safety, weather, sea conditions, government advisories, mechanical failure or force majeure. If the Libertad Whale Shark tour is cancelled before it starts for weather, safety or operational reasons, the guest may choose one of the following: a full refund of the whale shark ticket, a free rebooking within 7 days (subject to availability), or a transfer of the booking to another guest at no extra cost. Whale sharks are wild marine animals, so sightings are not guaranteed. If Libertad Whale Shark cancels the tour before it starts due to whale shark presence conditions, weather, safety or operational reasons, the guest may choose one of the following: a full refund of the whale shark ticket, a free rebooking within 7 days (subject to availability), or a transfer of the booking to another guest at no extra cost. If round-trip transportation has already been provided, only the whale shark ticket is refunded and the transportation fee is not refundable.
