Frequently Asked Questions FAQ

What makes VIP Fast Track different?

It's a fully private experience: an exclusive vehicle and a dedicated professional underwater photographer from Libertad accompany only your group, with no sharing. It also includes Fast Track entry, full gear (life jacket, fins, snorkel mask), luggage storage, bottled water, and a drop-off service.

How much does it cost?

₱5,820, the same price for both Philippine locals and foreign guests.

What's the cancellation and refund policy?

100% refund 15+ days before the tour, 80% at 8-14 days, 50% at 4-7 days, and no refund within 3 days.
