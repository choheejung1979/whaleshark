Frequently Asked Questions FAQ

What's included in the Regular Tour?

Admission + viewing + environmental fee, round-trip shared vehicle, local guide, premium fin rental, and a life jacket are included. Underwater photography, hotel drop-off, island tour, jumbo crab shrimp, and Fast Track entry are not included.

How much does it cost?

₱2,520 for Philippine locals and ₱2,820 for foreign guests.

Can I get a refund if I cancel?

A 100% refund is available if you cancel 15 or more days before the tour. Cancellations within 3 days are non-refundable.
