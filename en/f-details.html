Frequently Asked Questions FAQ

What's the difference between Fast Track and Regular?

Fast Track is an all-inclusive package with priority entry with minimal waiting, underwater photography (OSMO Action 5 Pro), luggage storage, bottled water, and a drop-off service after the tour. Regular provides only the essentials without these extras.

How much does it cost?

₱3,300 for Philippine locals and ₱3,600 for foreign guests.

What's the cancellation and refund policy?

100% refund 15+ days before the tour, 80% at 8-14 days, 50% at 4-7 days, and no refund within 3 days.
